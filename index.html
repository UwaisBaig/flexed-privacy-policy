Privacy Policy for Flexed

App: Flexed • Package: com.uwaisbaig.flexed • Developer: Uwais Baig

Last updated: October 2026

Flexed ("the app", package name: com.uwaisbaig.flexed), developed by Uwais Baig, is a daily number puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

Information we collect

Flexed does not require you to create an account, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any personally identifying information.

The app stores the following data locally on your device only, and this data is never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Your daily puzzle progress (in-progress attempts, the puzzle grid state)
  • Your game history and statistics (games played, win rate, streaks, attempt distribution)
  • Your language mode preference (Classic or Gen Z)
  • Whether you have completed the in-app tutorial

This local data stays on your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage.

Advertising

Flexed displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process certain data to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements, including:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (GAID)
  • General, non-precise technical, device, and interaction data used for ad delivery and fraud prevention

This data is collected and processed directly by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and partner practices. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and manage your ad settings, at:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Notifications

If you enable optional daily reminder notifications in the app's settings, these notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device via the Android system alarms. No notification data or device tokens are sent to us or any external server.

Children's privacy

Flexed is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal data with third parties. Third-party advertising data is collected directly by Google AdMob as described above under Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
